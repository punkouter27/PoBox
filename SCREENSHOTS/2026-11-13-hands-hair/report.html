2. Grandma — Pale Hair Fix (Before vs After)

Diagnosis: Inspection of Grandma.glb revealed two compounded causes: (1) Material_1 used emissiveFactor=[1,1,1] with emissiveTexture: texture_0, omitted metallicFactor (defaulting to 1.0 in glTF PBR), and set KHR_materials_specular.specularColorFactor=[2,2,2], blowing out highlights under lighting; (2) texture_0 (2048×2048) hair texels were washed-out pale blonde/tan.

Fix & Verification: Adjusted hair regions in texture_0 to rich warm chestnut while preserving face, teeth, ears, sweater, and trousers, and patched Material_1 (metallicFactor=0.0, roughnessFactor=0.88, emissiveFactor=[0.78,0.78,0.78], specularFactor=0.35, specularColorFactor=[1,1,1]) directly onto Grandma_orig.glb so all geometry, nodes, bones, and IBMs remain 100% identical (0.000000 IBM diff).

3. Kim: stretching arms (tried, not kept)

What was measured. Kim's clavicle bones (Shoulder.L/R) hold 20 to 40% of the weight on the first third of each upper arm, and SkinBinder does not drive the clavicles: they ride on the torso. Two reweights that hand that weight to UpperArm.L/R were rendered against the committed file, same poses, same cameras.

Result. Both reweights are worse. With more of the loose sleeve following the arm rigidly, its top outer corner swings out into a square shoulder pad. The committed weights give the narrowest, most natural sleeve in all three poses, so Assets/Models/Kim.glb was put back and is byte-identical to the committed file. The wider reweight is kept only as BLENDER/2026-11-13_kim/Kim_reweight_staged.glb.

Likely cause, outside the mesh. SkinBinder sizes the skin from the summed limb lengths, and its forearm aim measures the rig's elbow-to-glove distance (0.27 m on Kim) against the mesh's elbow-to-wrist distance. That makes the fitted scale about 6% too large for every boxer (Kim 1.652 where her height gives 1.554), and each bone is then placed on the rig's joint, so every limb segment is squeezed to 0.93 to 0.95 of its skinned length while the undriven clavicle and hand keep the larger scale. This was computed from the rig files and the meshes, not seen in Unity. It applies to all eight boxers, so it does not by itself explain why only Kim was reported; her loose T-shirt sleeves are the part of her mesh most sensitive to it. Not fixable in Blender without changing the skeleton.
