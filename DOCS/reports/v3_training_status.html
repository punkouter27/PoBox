What is happening

Each boxer got a new body: a waist that bends 60° forward and 30° back like a person's, and body parts weighed the way real people's are (heavier thighs, a lighter trunk). Its old brain was learned on the old body, so each boxer goes back to school in three lessons: walking (30 min), getting up (25 min), then boxing against the seven others (45 min). After the walking and boxing lessons it sits an exam in the same physics the game uses. Nothing changes in the game until a boxer passes.

The three numbers that matter

ToddlerLine near the floor = the boxer doesn't fall over. ChildEvery minute of walking and turning, how often it tripped. The new body has to learn to balance again; a flat line near zero means it has. Adultenv/falls_per_min of the footwork run, all commands mixed (stand, walk, turn, shoves). The footwork exam then checks stand/walk/turn separately in C MuJoCo 3.5.0.
ToddlerHigher = it gets back up after falling. ChildOut of every knockdown in practice, the share it stood up from on its own. The exam wants 8 in 10 within 8 seconds. Adultenv/NAME_up_rate in the get-up run (standing still 1.5 s within the episode), trained against a frozen partner; the hand-over bank then saves its last standing poses for the boxing lesson.
ToddlerHigher = it hits the other boxer more. ChildPunches that actually land each second. The exam asks for at least one every two seconds (0.5), and fast ones (4 m/s). Adultenv/hits_per_s over the gauntlet (all seven opponents mixed). The run stops early if it holds 1.2/s with few falls and varied punches; otherwise at 45 min.
