## Rules while working

- **Parity rule:** if a boxer stands in C MuJoCo and falls in Unity, stop training and fix the cause first.
- One GPU job at a time. Do not start one while another session's run is on the GPU.
- Every training run: clear obsolete TensorBoard runs, start TensorBoard and the viewer, log the run and the
  decision in `rl_optimization_log.md`. Runs over 30 minutes: close the Unity editor, and write the HTML chart
  report (three charts, three reading levels) that `AGENTS.md` asks for.
- Scenes and prefabs are authored in the editor through the Unity CLI or MCP, not generated by code.
- A UI change gets an annotated before/after screenshot in an HTML report.
- Tick a task when it is done and verified. Commit to `master` at the end of each numbered group.

## Exam lines and pass marks

The boxing exam (`training/tools/exam.py`, C MuJoCo 3.5.0) has seven lines: footing, attack, guard, chin, getting
up, carrying on, and under pressure (since 2026-11-03: at least 0.25 punches landed a second against the boxer's
hardest opponent). The footwork exam is `training/tools/footwork_c.py --exam`.

| Rung | Behaviour | Pass mark |
|---|---|---|
| R0 | Stand | upright 20 s under 10-30 N s shoves and 1 kg cubes at 5 m/s, 95% of episodes |
| R1 | Walk | tracks 0.3-1.0 m/s within 0.15 m/s over 10 s; at most 0.2 falls a minute |
| Gate | Unity parity | replay output difference under 1e-4; 5 s closed loop: upright in both, cadence within 10%, RMS torque within 15%; fall rate within 0.05 of C MuJoCo over 25 episodes |
| R2 | Turn | opponent anywhere in 360 degrees; faces within 15 degrees inside 3 s, 90% |
| R3 | Get up | standing unaided 1.5 s within 8 s, 80% (60% within 9 s for the grandparents) |
| R4 | Attack | at least 0.5 punches landed a second at 4 m/s times the boxer's speed factor |
| R5 | Guard | stops at least 30% of head shots, or takes under 0.3 a second |
| R6 | Chin | legs taken at most 1.5 times a minute under the daze rule |
| R7 | Carrying on, footing | up 3 s after a get-up, 90%; at most 0.2 unprovoked falls a minute against all opponents |

Every rung also has to keep each joint's 99th-percentile speed under its human limit.

## Phase F: the free-for-all (owner, 2026-10-15)

The free-for-all (`Assets/Scenes/Melee.unity`, `FreeForAll`) runs the 1-on-1 brains in a crowd: each sees one
opponent (the nearest on its feet), every body is solid, and bodies pile. Bumping into a third boxer (never seen
in training) makes them fall, and a get-up policy taught with one opponent standing clear often cannot rise beside
other bodies. The game works round it. Training is the real fix. The owner's ask: **a motivation to seek out where
the closest opponent is and move forward to attack.**

- [ ] F1 `envs/boxing.py`: a crowd mode, K opponents per learner (frozen shipped match policies, 3 to 7 of them),
      bodies solid, in the 6.1 m ring. The learner observes its nearest opponent on its feet (the game's rule, same
      103 numbers); the target is re-picked every control step with the game's switch margin (0.4 m).
- [ ] F2 *(flag in, 2026-10-16: `--seek-w`, measured against the one opponent)* Reward for closing on the nearest
      opponent on its feet when it is outside punching range, plus the usual hit, guard and fall terms. Charge
      contact with a third body only through the falls it causes.
- [ ] F3 *(first half in, 2026-10-16: `--ropes-start`)* Episodes start as the game's rounds do: every boxer at the
      ropes facing in; and a share from mid-melee states (a bank like the handover bank, from game runs).
- [ ] F4 Get-up in a crowd: `envs/getup.py` with bodies lying beside and over the learner, so a get-up policy
      learns to climb out of a pile.
- [ ] F5 Exam: the game's free-for-all in C MuJoCo, 20 bouts of 3 rounds: slips per boxer per minute, get-ups
      beaten the count, punches landed per minute; pass marks to be set from the first run.
- [ ] G11 Free-for-all slips in the first 20 s: needs the crowd training above.
- [ ] R12 Train boxers to step round bodies on the canvas: `--bodies N` built and staged
      (`training/queues/queue_melee_staged.json`); `--obs-bodies` exists in `train_gauntlet.py`. Not run on v3.
